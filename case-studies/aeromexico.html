Define the Problems

When flights were delayed, canceled, or disrupted, travelers had to rely on agents, call centers, and manual support to understand what they could do next. This created three major problems:

CX Problem 1:
Customers Had to Chase Information

During disruptions, travelers had to actively search for flight updates, available options, and compensation details across multiple channels.

CX Problem 2:
Disconnected Support Touchpoints

Flight updates, rebooking, hotel support, compensation, refunds, and wallet credits were not connected in one clear mobile journey.

CX Problem 3:
Unclear Eligibility and Next Steps

Travelers needed clearer guidance on what changed, what they qualified for, and what action to take next.

Design Solutions

Based on the three CX problems, we defined three design principles to guide the travel disruption self-service experience.

Solution 1

Proactive Support

Send real-time updates when a disruption happens. Recommend the best available rebooking option first. Give travelers flexible self-service options when they need to make changes. Guide users toward the next best action instead of making them search for help.

  • Send real-time updates when a disruption happens
  • Recommend the best available rebooking option first
  • Give travelers flexible self-service options when they need to make changes
  • Guide users toward the next best action instead of making them search for help

Solution 2

Connected Journey

Bring flight updates, rebooking, hotel support, compensation, and refunds into one mobile journey. Create smoother handoffs across app, airport, call center, and partner services.

  • Bring flight updates, rebooking, hotel support, compensation, and refunds into one mobile journey
  • Create smoother handoffs across app, airport, call center, and partner services
  • Allow travelers to continue through their preferred support channel when needed
  • Connect wallet vouchers and airport service partners into the same service flow
Placeholder: Solution 2 Image (1268x400px)
Solution 3

Trusted Guidance

Use consistent information across channels from a single source of truth. Show clear IROP flight status and eligibility information. Explain what travelers qualify for and what action to take next. Use reassuring, solution-oriented messaging during stressful moments.

  • Use consistent information across channels from a single source of truth
  • Show clear IROP flight status and eligibility information
  • Explain what travelers qualify for and what action to take next
  • Use reassuring, solution-oriented messaging during stressful moments

Impact

The redesigned experience helped Aeroméxico move key disruption recovery actions from manual and paper-based processes into mobile self-service.

We enabled customers to rebook flights, access hotel support, and claim compensation in-app. This digitized compensation and refund flows that previously relied on more manual processes and reduced call center dependency for eligible disruption scenarios.
